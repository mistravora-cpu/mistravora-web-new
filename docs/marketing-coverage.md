## Module limits and setup

- Apply `supabase/migrations/0039_marketing_platform.sql` after the existing migrations. It was tested locally; it has not been applied to the remote database by this task. Existing contact submissions retain a pre-migration fallback.
- Booking uses administrator-created slots and reserves them atomically. ICS downloads work with calendar applications, including Google Calendar import. There is no Google Calendar OAuth/sync connection, recurring availability generator, or automated meeting URL provider.
- CRM extends inquiries with qualification, pipeline stage, internal notes, deal value and consented campaign attribution. It does not infer real revenue or automatically score a person from browsing behavior.
- Email campaigns are drafts until an administrator schedules them. The authenticated worker uses the existing Resend delivery API, requires a verified EMAIL_FROM, RESEND_API_KEY, EMAIL_TOKEN_SECRET (32+ characters), CRON_SECRET and SUPABASE_SERVICE_ROLE_KEY. No email was sent during development.
- The worker snapshots the full subscriber audience, checks active subscriptions and campaign pauses between sends, claims deliveries atomically, locks content after delivery starts, and uses provider idempotency keys. Pausing cannot retract a request already in flight. Failed/uncertain sends require inspection; they are not blindly replayed. A cron scheduler must call the endpoint. Complex nurture sequences, arbitrary webhook destinations, Google Calendar sync and autonomous proposals are not configured.
- Content revisions are stored for administrative audit. Automatic restore/merge, generated editorial content, invented testimonials and machine-authored business claims are not published.
- SEO overrides apply to public index pages and supported content detail pages. Redirects are reviewed in `src/data/redirects.json` and take effect on deployment.
- Real names, author biographies, testimonials, translations, legal policies, client results, downloadable assets and videos must be provided or published by the business. The site does not fabricate them.
