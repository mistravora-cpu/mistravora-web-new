## How to read this document

Each field has one status. A status on a subsection applies to all fields explicitly grouped beneath it.

- **CONFIRMED**: supplied by Husni or explicitly established in the supplied project context.
- **DRAFTED FROM CONFIRMED INFORMATION**: wording based on those facts, without adding new business promises.
- **RECOMMENDED — NEEDS APPROVAL**: a proposed editorial choice, workflow, scope, or policy structure; not an existing company commitment.
- **NEED FROM OWNER**: a factual answer, business decision, permission, or evidence is missing.
- **NOT APPLICABLE**: no owner content input is required for this item in the present scope.
- **KEEP UNPUBLISHED**: excluded from the proposed publishing batch until substantiated. This label does not mean an existing live record has already been unpublished.

Recommended features are possible project scope, not included features in every quotation. No new company results, credentials, testimonials, employee history, prices, or product capabilities are asserted here.
